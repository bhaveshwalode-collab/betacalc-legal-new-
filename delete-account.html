Account & Data Deletion

App: BetaCalc  |  Developer: Bhavesh Walode

This page explains how users of the BetaCalc app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request that your BetaCalc account and its associated data be deleted, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to bhaveshwalode@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in to BetaCalc.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - BetaCalc".
  3. In the email, mention that you want your account and all associated data deleted.
  4. We will verify your request and delete your account and associated data within 30 days of receiving it.

Requesting partial data deletion

If you want to delete only certain data (such as your study progress) without deleting your entire account, mention this in your email and specify what you would like removed.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Data Action
Account information (name, email, profile) Deleted
Study progress and app activity data Deleted
Crash and diagnostic logs Deleted

What data may be retained

We may retain limited information only where required by law, or for fraud prevention and security purposes, for a maximum additional retention period of 90 days, after which it is permanently deleted. Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained for analytics.

Contact

For any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at bhaveshwalode@gmail.com.
